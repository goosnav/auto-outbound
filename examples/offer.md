# Example offer — replace before use

I build a small tool that turns emailed quote requests into a review queue.
It flags missing drawings, materials, quantities, and delivery dates.
The customer's team retains pricing, technical, and order approval.

## Good customer

A business with enough repeated intake work to justify a small implementation.
The actual volume, systems, bottleneck, buying authority and economics must be
confirmed with the customer. A public RFQ form does not prove a problem exists.

## First step

A short process conversation to decide whether a bounded implementation makes sense.

## Proof and limits

This is a sample, not a claim of delivered results. Add only your real experience,
case studies, capabilities and exclusions. Do not promise savings or integration
with a named system unless you can support that claim.
